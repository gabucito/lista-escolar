Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high-fidelity, functional desktop application screenshot for Lista Escolar, a Spanish-language Chilean school supply app. This redesign must solve the specific problem that the supply catalog is cramped and barely visible.
Composition: a single flat landscape desktop screen, approximately 1440 by 900 proportions, filling the image edge to edge. No device frame, decorative illustration, marketing hero, or large empty margins. Use a compact navy #24374A app header, warm off-white #F8F7F2 canvas, white panels, pale sage #E8EEDC section backgrounds, green #356552 primary actions, clear 16px-style sans-serif form labels, restrained small serif page titles. High contrast and 44px touchable controls.
Top 56px navigation: open-book line icon and "Lista Escolar"; "Buscar listas", "Mis listas"; small user menu "Camila". Below it a compact one-line school summary: "Colegio Los Aromos · 5° básico A · 2027", small "Editar datos", a quiet green "Guardado", secondary "Vista previa" and primary "Publicar lista". Metadata consumes at most 70px; do not show a large metadata form.
MAIN WORKSPACE: two broad, equal-height columns filling the remaining viewport. The LEFT catalog uses about 55% width. Header "Agregar útiles"; full-width prominent search input "Buscar por nombre o característica"; one compact row with category dropdown "Todas las categorías" and subcategory dropdown "Todas". Keep search and filters pinned above results. At least EIGHT complete catalog rows must be visible at once. Each row shows a readable generic supply name, concise specifications in a secondary line, and an explicit "+ Agregar" button at right. Example rows: "Cuaderno universitario" / "100 hojas · Cuadriculado 7 mm"; "Cuaderno de composición" / "80 hojas · Línea"; "Lápiz grafito" / "HB · Sin goma"; "Goma de borrar"; "Lápices de colores" / "12 colores"; "Pegamento en barra" / "40 g"; "Regla" / "30 cm"; "Tijera escolar" / "Punta redonda"; "Block de dibujo" / "Tamaño carta". This must look like a usable catalog, never a narrow sidebar. Bottom subtle utilities "+ Útil personalizado" and "Importar Excel o CSV".
RIGHT column about45%: heading "Mi lista" and "6 útiles". A compact editable table/cards with columns "Útil", "Cantidad", "Unidad". Show six distinct requirements: Cuaderno universitario /4/unidad; Lápiz grafito /3/unidad; Goma de borrar /2/unidad; Lápices de colores /1/caja; Pegamento en barra /2/unidad; Regla /1/unidad. Each row has a small drag handle, clear quantity control, unit dropdown, restrained remove icon with adequate target area; expandable "Nota" rather than exposing every optional field. Include one open short note "Forro del color que prefieras." Show a subtle nonblocking confirmation below the list "Regla agregada · Deshacer".
Design actual task UI with realistic density and legible Spanish text. List and catalog must remain visible together. No prices, shopping cart, checkout, Gmail, AI button, school verification badge, or fake official endorsement.
