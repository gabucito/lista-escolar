Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high-fidelity Spanish-language desktop web application design proposal for Lista Escolar.
Input images 1 and 2 are references for the existing app's brand, content and category-to-attribute behavior; redesign them substantially for usability, without copying their awkward spacing or letter placeholders. Output one beautifully crisp, flat front-on UI design board, landscape approximately 1920 by 1280. It should look implementable, with legible Spanish text and strong contrast, not a photograph of a laptop.
The main app frame occupies at least 85% of the board. A very small separate annotation strip BELOW the frame shows the initial left-panel state with category choices Cuadernos, Escritura, Arte y dibujo, Geometría, and a right arrow captioned "El mismo panel cambia a características". This strip is a design annotation, NOT a second navigation pane within the app.
Critical interaction invariant: ONE left sidenav. It starts with category choices and, after choosing Cuadernos, THE SAME panel shows its attributes. The main screenshot shows this selected-category state. It contains a permanent clearly clickable "← Categorías" above the scrolling filters. No subcategory navigation, no second category sidebar, no horizontal category tabs. The center shows results; the right shows the populated working list. All three remain visible together.
Keep the established navy, forest-green, warm off-white and light sage brand family. Small original line icons for school supplies replace large initial letters. No store brands, prices, cart or checkout. No decorative hero, huge empty state or confusing extra navigation.
Slim global header: book mark "Lista Escolar", "Buscar listas", "Mis listas", a discreet user menu. Single compact editing toolbar: "Colegio Los Aromos · 5° básico A · 2027", "Editar datos", "Guardado", "Vista previa", green primary "Publicar lista".
Selected category Cuadernos. Left attributes: "Tipo de cuaderno" with Cuadriculado selected, Rayado, Composición, Croquis, Liso; "Formato" with Universitario selected and College, Medio oficio; optional "Hojas" choices 60, 80, 100; optional "Cuadrícula" choices 5 mm, 7 mm. Fit the four groups comfortably without tiny type. Type and size use single-select semantics. Category title and back control have a clear hierarchy.
Center: search "Buscar útiles o características"; title "Cuadernos"; active removable chips "Cuadriculado ×" and "Universitario ×"; "Limpiar"; "5 útiles". Five distinct products, exactly these requirements: 100 hojas / 7 mm; 100 hojas / 5 mm; 80 hojas / 7 mm; 80 hojas / 5 mm; 60 hojas / 7 mm. These are all Cuaderno universitario cuadriculado. Keep readable product titles but avoid repeating every specification in both title and description. Each has an explicit add action.
Right panel: "Mi lista · 3 útiles". Three compact entries, fully visible: "Cuaderno universitario" subtitle "100 hojas · 7 mm", quantity 2; "Lápiz grafito", quantity 2; "Goma de borrar", quantity 1. Small minus/quantity/plus control and remove icon per entry; unobtrusive "Nota" expansion. No need for tall form fields for every row. Footer has quiet "Guardado". Center bottom secondary actions "Útil personalizado" and "Importar Excel o CSV".
Avoid garbled words, invented statistics, repeated logos, chopped controls, low-contrast text, overcrowding and excessive card nesting. This is a design proposal, not a claim of shipped functionality.

Direction B — Comparar y agregar. Put the title "B · Comparar y agregar" in the small presentation margin outside the app. This direction emphasizes fast comparison and efficient scanning, with a clean professional workspace and mostly white surfaces, a restrained navy top bar and green actions. The main center is a single comparison LIST/TABLE, NOT cards. It has aligned column headings "Útil", "Hojas", "Cuadrícula", and an unlabeled action column; five generous but compact rows. Each row includes a tiny notebook icon, concise "Cuaderno universitario" and secondary "Cuadriculado", a clear numeric sheet value (100,100,80,80,60), grid value (7 mm,5 mm,7 mm,5 mm,7 mm), and a consistent green "+ Agregar". Use subtle row dividers and a pale-green hover/active indication on the first row, not spreadsheet grid clutter. Clear alignment and readable values make differences obvious. The left sidenav has compact checkable option rows, visible groups and a strong back-to-categories control; the right working list is compact with inline quantity steppers. All five comparisons and all three working-list entries fit without page scrolling. The visual is precise, quiet and useful, with smaller masthead and ample central comparison width.
